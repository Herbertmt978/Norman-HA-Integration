## Highlights

- Uses discovered room levels and one-second pacing by default for whole-room
  Open, Close, and position commands.
- Falls back to a native room broadcast only when no usable levels are exposed.
- Adds **Configure > Room commands** so individually verified rooms can opt into
  simultaneous native Open and Close broadcasts.
- Keeps non-native endpoints and position-slider commands on exact level fan-out,
  even when simultaneous broadcasts are enabled for a room.

## Upgrade notes

- No configuration or entity migration is required.
- Existing rooms use reliable level fan-out after upgrading. Open **Configure >
  Room commands** to opt an individually verified room into simultaneous native
  Open and Close broadcasts.
- Open and Close position profiles, entity IDs, room and panel devices, battery
  sensors, and automations remain unchanged.

## Diagnostics and branding

- Adds privacy-safe room, window, level, group, model, and effective command-mode
  diagnostics.
- Adds matching entity attributes and debug logs so accepted commands can be
  correlated without exposing credentials or raw hub payloads.
- Continues to bundle the validated Norman icon and logo for Home Assistant and
  HACS clients that support local custom-integration brands. HACS 2.0.5 may show
  a placeholder until its upstream local-brand fix is released.
