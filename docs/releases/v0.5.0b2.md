# 0.5.0b2 — guided RF learning and saved profiles

With bridge firmware 0.10.0, the ESPHome RF setup flow can start from an empty
board. Learn a motorised section by pressing its original controller's Open and
Close actions, confirm what moved, then assign it to a named HA room. The room
and its sections get standard Open/Close controls. No hub credentials are needed.

Reconfigure adds more profiles and lets you rename, change rooms or remove a
selected profile with confirmation. Removal affects that bridge's record and
its bound HA controls, not other bridges or the original Norman hardware.
Update automations using removed or renamed room entities.

Forwarding-only controller actions have a separate learning path, including
native room actions whose direct rolling-code generation remains unqualified.
Older bridge firmware retains manual commissioning and room selection.

The paired firmware keeps full direct bursts and waits two seconds after each
completed burst before sending up to two unchanged repeats. This increases
delivery attempts but is not physical feedback or a delivery guarantee.

Continuous position/tilt sliders and Stop remain unsupported. The RF path only
generates learned endpoints; its assumed HA state must not be treated as a
measured opening. Existing Gen1 and ShadeAuto hub transports are unchanged.

Follow the [RF setup guide](../esphome-rf.md) and the bridge's linked instruction
manual. This remains an experimental pre-release, not general compatibility
qualification for every Norman installation.
