# Norman v0.5.0 — Gen 2 rooms, hub reliability and RF room fix

This stable release replaces v0.4.1 and the v0.5.0 betas. Update through HACS and
restart Home Assistant. Existing Gen 1 and Gen 2 entries, entity IDs and settings
are kept; no reconfiguration is required.

## Gen 2 (ShadeAuto)

- Each discovered room gets its own device with a **Shades** cover. Individual
  shades remain available. Room commands preflight every member, then move each
  shade in turn, keeping its other rail. They are not a native room broadcast.
- Control requests to one hub are spaced at least 750 ms apart and carry distinct
  timestamps and task IDs. This addresses a likely cause of HA cover groups moving
  only one shade (issue #25).
- Room IDs sent as numeric strings are accepted, and missing-room diagnostics are
  available. This addresses a likely cause of room devices not appearing (#26).
- Bottom-only shades no longer show tilt or move a second rail.
- An optional, disabled-by-default **Battery reading** sensor shows the hub's raw
  value without assuming units.

The maintainer owns a Gen 1 hub only. Gen 2 behaviour is covered by automated
tests, not yet confirmed on physical Gen 2 hardware. Please report results in
issues #25 and #26.

## Gen 1

- Transient login HTTP 500 responses get bounded retries, and the last good
  snapshot is kept for up to two failed polls.

## Experimental ESPHome RF bridge

- Room covers now send each section as its own command, one after another. A
  four-section room takes about 25 seconds. Sending sections together in one
  burst intermittently left panels unmoved; in camera-checked trials, sequential
  commands moved all 24 of 24 lounge panel movements.
- If a section's command fails, that section becomes uncertain and later sections
  in the room are not sent. Nothing is retried automatically.
- Timeouts name the failing operation and elapsed time. A failed status check
  before a command sends nothing and keeps the previous assumed positions.

The RF transport remains experimental and reports assumed, not measured, positions.
See the [RF guide](../esphome-rf.md). It works with any bridge firmware that has
the native protocol 3 actions; firmware 0.11.4-experimental is the current release.

## Rolling back

In HACS, open Norman's three-dot menu, choose **Redownload**, then **Need a
different version?**, select **v0.4.1** and restart Home Assistant. Gen 2 room
devices and RF entries are not used by v0.4.1.
