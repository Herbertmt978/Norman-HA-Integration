# v0.5.0b1 — experimental ESPHome RF controls

Adds a separate transport for a commissioned
[Norman RF Bridge](https://github.com/Herbertmt978/norman-rf-bridge), without
calling a Norman hub. Existing Gen1 and ShadeAuto entries remain independent.

Select a discovered ESPHome bridge and the complete rooms it will control.
The integration exposes individual section and whole-room Open/Close covers,
uses each learned preferred closing direction, and checks commissioning
identities before sending. Each room may contain at most eight sections.
One learned profile may be bound to only one direct transmitter in this HA
instance; other bridges may still repeat its traffic.

State is assumed, not measured. Reload starts with unknown positions. A failed
burst is not retried by HA or sent through the hub. Firmware 0.9.2 can repeat
a successfully transmitted command in the background, using unchanged frames
and a bounded budget; its own switch controls that policy. Native ESPHome
protocol3 response support is required in addition to the integration's ordinary
HA2024.11 hub-transport minimum. See [setup and limits](../esphome-rf.md).

Camera-observed firmware0.9 tests passed five office sections and four lounge
panels individually and as room batches from local bridges. Original Norman
repeaters remained powered. This is not whole-house reliability, exclusive
range-extension or secured customer-product qualification.

Subsequent HA-cover trials missed sections in both rooms, including a completed
sequential two-room run. Unattended schedule qualification has not passed;
the initial successful pairs must not be read as a reliability guarantee.
The owner has chosen to start using direct ESP sunrise/sunset control anyway:
the existing automation is now enabled with local office/lounge RF targets,
unchanged sun triggers and a saved rollback configuration. Phone-app schedules
are unchanged. This operational choice does not change the qualification result.

Before moving schedules, test the actual HA covers, preserve the old automation
configuration, and replace its room targets without changing its timing.
Disable corresponding schedules in the Norman app only after the replacement
works, to avoid duplicate or conflicting commands. App schedules are not
automatically discovered or disabled by this release.
